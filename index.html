About

I am a Biotechnology graduate with a keen interest in omics and computational algorithms applicable to biological data. My work brings together large-scale omics data analysis, visualization and quality control to connect computational results with biological insights.

With training and experience in Python, R and data visualization, I am interested in automating workflows, ensuring data reliability and contributing to scientific discoveries. I enjoy working both collaboratively and independently.
